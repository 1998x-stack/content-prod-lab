# WeChat Layout & Image Reference

## Mobile-first article layout

- Paragraphs should usually occupy 1–4 mobile lines.
- Add whitespace before/after major turns, reveals, and punch lines.
- Use bold selectively for conclusions, decisive numbers, reversals, and key claims.
- Use roughly 3–6 natural subheads for a 2500–4500-character article.
- Subheads should feel like narrative turns, e.g. “真正的开关，其实在两年前”, not “一、背景介绍”.

## Image planning

Typical 2500–4500 Chinese-character article: roughly 4–8 images **when they add information**.

Every image should do at least one job:
- establish the scene
- explain geography
- identify a person/subject/product
- show scale
- support historical context
- explain a number/trend
- create a meaningful visual transition

### Preferred image types

1. **Cover candidate** — clear subject, strong composition, tolerant of WeChat cover cropping, little/no embedded text.
2. **News scene** — establishes reality and scale.
3. **Key subject/person/product/building** — when a central actor needs visual identity.
4. **Map** — borders, routes, enclaves, war, international relations, logistics.
5. **Historical material** — old photos, maps, archival documents.
6. **Data chart** — only if a key change/trend is central.

## Image authenticity and rights

Prefer:
- official government/institution images
- public agency materials
- clearly licensed/public-domain assets
- Wikimedia Commons where appropriate
- corporate official press kits/newsrooms
- self-generated charts, diagrams, or maps

Retain source/credit information in the document.

Do not disguise AI-generated content as a real news scene. AI visuals may be used only as obvious illustrations/concepts and should be labeled when necessary.

## Captions

A caption should explain **why the visual matters to the argument**. Avoid captions that merely repeat visible content.
