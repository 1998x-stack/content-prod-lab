# WeChat editorial standard

## Editorial objective

Produce a Chinese long-form article that combines strategic-research rigor with mobile readability.

Tone: professional, analytical, clear, restrained, non-propagandistic.

## Title

Use a strong question, paradox, or structural insight.

Good pattern:

- `真正决定X的，不是A，而是B`
- `X正在进入怎样的阶段？`
- `为什么X比地图上看起来更重要？`

Avoid clickbait, emotional certainty, or sensational predictions.

Add a subtitle that states the analytical dimensions.

## Lead

150–300 Chinese characters.

Start with:

- a strategic contradiction,
- a counterintuitive fact,
- a map problem,
- a decisive number,
- or a change in the underlying structure.

Do not open with a long historical chronology.

## Core judgment block

Near the top, provide 3–6 compact conclusions under `核心判断`.

A reader who stops here should understand the thesis, current balance, and main uncertainty.

## Mobile paragraph rhythm

- One idea per paragraph.
- Usually 2–4 sentences per paragraph.
- Avoid dense walls of text.
- Use standalone key judgments sparingly.
- Use bold for genuinely important claims only.

## Section architecture

Prefer numbered, argumentative headings such as:

- `一、问题真正是什么？`
- `二、先看地图：为什么这里重要？`
- `三、力量到底在谁手里？`
- `四、各方真正想要什么？`
- `五、为什么会走到今天？`
- `六、不同理论告诉我们什么？`
- `七、真正决定局势的是哪些变量？`
- `八、谁拥有战术、战略和长期优势？`
- `九、未来可能出现的三种路径`
- `十、以后看新闻，真正要盯什么？`

Adapt rather than mechanically copying all headings.

## Information modules

Use occasional modules such as:

- `【核心判断】`
- `【一个容易产生的误解】`
- `【地缘政治视角】`
- `【关键变量】`
- `【值得观察】`
- `【风险提示】`

Do not overuse them.

## Tables

Because WeChat is mobile-first:

- keep tables to roughly 3–5 columns,
- avoid wide matrices,
- convert overly wide comparisons into cards or short subsections.

## Maps and graphics

Maps outrank decorative photos for geopolitical analysis.

A visual should answer one question, for example:

- Where is the chokepoint?
- How are alliances arranged?
- Where are logistics corridors?
- How does trade/energy flow?
- How has a trend changed over time?

Prefer self-created diagrams, official/open-use assets, or clearly licensed sources. Do not embed uncertain-copyright news photos in a publication-ready file.

## Quotes

Use short quotations only when wording itself matters. Prefer paraphrase for normal evidence.

## Ending

Do not merely repeat the opening.

Answer:

1. `这个事件真正争夺的是什么？`
2. `最终结果最可能由什么变量决定？`

Then give 5–8 monitoring indicators so readers can distinguish signal from noise.

## Length guidance

- quick analysis: ~3,000–5,000 Chinese characters,
- deep analysis: ~6,000–10,000,
- special report: ~10,000–18,000.

Use the shortest length that fully supports the argument.
