# Fact Integrity During Video Conversion

## Source article is not automatically infallible

Treat the completed article as the primary content source, but preserve a verification mindset.

Research/verify when:
- the user asks for latest/current updates
- a claim is time-sensitive and may have changed
- the source contains a surprising legal/political/scientific claim that is essential to the video
- a causal claim is stronger than the evidence shown
- a number appears internally inconsistent
- additional visual sourcing requires factual context

If the source already has strong citations and no update is requested, do not re-research every minor detail merely for formality.

## Compression must preserve epistemic status

Do not convert:

- `可能是` → `就是`
- `有观点认为` → `事实是`
- `A之后B发生` → `A导致B`
- `不能使用某一程序` → `行为合法`

The shorter script must remain as accurate as the longer article.

## Key claims

Keep at least the facts needed to support:
- opening hook
- major reversal
- core mechanism
- final conclusion

If one of these depends on a disputed premise, qualify or change the structure.

## Severe topics

For war, mass death, severe disasters, violence, or minors harmed:
- lower joke density
- avoid humorous sound effects around harm
- do not use victims as spectacle
- focus criticism on decisions, institutions, responsibility, or system failures
